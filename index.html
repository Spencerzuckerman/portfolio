About Me

My name is Spencer Zuckerman, and I’m from Tenafly, NJ. I’m currently a freshman at Indiana University studying Elementary Education. I decided to study this because I adore working with children and learning how the child's brain functions. In the future, I would love to be a Kindergarten teacher. One of the things that interests me most about my major is learning all of the behind-the-scenes aspects of becoming a teacher, and I’m so excited to learn more throughout college. Outside of school, I enjoy going on long walks, playing tennis, and finding new movies to watch. I also love spending time with my family and friends. Something that has been a big part of my life is working with children. I have spent years babysitting and have also had opportunities to work with children in classrooms and through volunteering. These experiences have made me even more confident that teaching is what I want to do. I would describe myself as caring, outgoing, and determined. When it comes to reading and writing, I enjoy them most when I am interested in the topic and can connect it to my own life. I sometimes struggle with getting my ideas organized when writing, so this semester I hope to become more confident in my writing process. Since starting at IU, I have been most excited about meeting new people, becoming more independent, and beginning classes related to my future career. I’m excited to see what the year brings. .
